About This Platform

Empowering Citizens Through Innovation and Collaboration

What is this website?

The Jharkhand Societal Challenge Platform is a digital initiative designed to bridge the gap between the citizens of Jharkhand and the state's top academic institutions. It serves as a centralized hub where local issues are transformed into structured, solvable challenges.

What is the use of this website?

  • Report Local Issues: Citizens can submit detailed societal challenges—such as waterlogging, healthcare shortages, or sanitation problems—complete with photo/video evidence and GPS location.
  • Smart Auto-Matching: Our platform automatically analyzes the category of the problem and routes it to the exact University Department (like NIT Jamshedpur or RIMS Ranchi) best equipped to solve it.
  • Emergency Priority: Life-threatening issues can be flagged as "URGENT," ensuring they are sorted to the top of the queue for immediate attention.

How does it help?

By collecting real-time data with location tags and risk scores, this platform helps the government and industry identify blind spots. It fosters a collaborative ecosystem where academic research meets ground reality, leading to faster, data-driven solutions for the people of Jharkhand.
